Five Star Agencies started as a local supplier of household vessels and has grown into a small catalogue of furniture, electronics, and everyday steel and brass ware. We work directly with manufacturers and trusted wholesalers, so we can keep prices fair without cutting corners on quality.

Most of our customers order and get support through WhatsApp — it's faster than email and lets us send photos, confirm sizes, and sort out delivery details in one conversation. We check every electronics item before it ships, and we stand behind our furniture and vessels with a straightforward returns process.

We're a small operation, which means real people answer your messages — not a script.
