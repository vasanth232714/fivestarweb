Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Five Star Agencies ("we", "us", "our") sells electronics, furniture and household vessels, and we communicate with customers through this website, email, and WhatsApp. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly, and some information automatically when you contact or order from us.

  • Contact details: name, phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, and delivery address.
  • Order details: items purchased, order value, payment status, and delivery preferences.
  • Message content: the messages you send us on WhatsApp, email, or the contact form, so we can respond to orders and support questions.
  • Basic technical data if you browse this site, such as pages viewed, using standard web server logs.

How we use WhatsApp

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform, provided by Meta, to send order confirmations, delivery updates, and to answer customer questions. When you message us on WhatsApp, Meta processes that message to deliver it to us, under Meta's own privacy policy and WhatsApp's terms. We do not use your WhatsApp conversations for advertising, and we do not sell your phone number or message content to third parties.

How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To respond to questions and provide customer support.
  • To send order-related updates (confirmations, dispatch, delivery, and issue resolution).
  • To keep basic records for accounting, warranty, and legal compliance.
  • To improve our catalogue and service based on aggregated, non-identifying trends.

We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

Sharing your information

We share information only where needed to run the business:

  • With delivery and logistics partners, so orders can be shipped to you.
  • With payment processors, to complete and verify payments.
  • With Meta / WhatsApp, as the platform used to exchange messages with you.
  • With our website host (Netlify), which processes contact-form submissions so we can receive them.
  • With authorities, where required by law.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Data retention

We keep order and communication records for as long as needed to fulfil orders, handle warranty or support issues, and meet accounting and legal obligations, after which we delete or anonymise the data.

Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us what personal information we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • You can ask us to delete your information, subject to legal and accounting requirements.
  • You can stop messaging us on WhatsApp at any time; you may also block or report our business number directly within WhatsApp.

To exercise any of these choices, contact us using the details below.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect the information we hold, including limiting access to staff who need it to do their job. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.

Children

Our products and services are intended for adults making purchases. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest version.

Contact us

For any questions about this policy or your information, contact us at contact@fivestaragencies.shop or via WhatsApp on our listed business number.
